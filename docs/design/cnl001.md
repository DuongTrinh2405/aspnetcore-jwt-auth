# CNL001 Visual Style Baseline

`cnl001` is the current product UI direction for CNL Service.

## Personality

- Bright, friendly, operational, trustworthy.
- Modern SaaS marketplace for camera, Wi-Fi, internet and light electrical services.
- Mobile-first, card-based, clear actions, simple forms for non-technical Vietnamese users.

## Layout Principles

- Keep current structure: sidebar/admin cockpit, customer dashboard, technician job feed, mobile bottom tabs.
- Use generous spacing and strong visual hierarchy.
- Avoid dark cyber-tech styling, flashy gradients, decorative blobs, dense tables, and generic CRUD styling.
- Cards are rounded, elevated softly, and separated with subtle borders.

## Palette Direction

- Bright blue primary.
- Cyan accents for realtime/service cues.
- White cards on a fresh light gray background.
- Slate text with soft borders and calm shadows.
- Status colors remain semantic: emerald, amber, rose, cyan/blue.

## Component Rules

- Buttons: high-contrast, rounded, slight lift on hover, clear disabled state.
- Inputs: calm white surface, strong focus ring, readable size.
- Cards: white surface, thin border, layered but subtle shadow.
- Sidebar: glassy white, active nav bright blue, quiet blue-tinted hover states.
- Charts/reports: simple bars and KPI blocks, not chart-heavy.

## Current Token Name

Use `cnl001` when referring to this visual style in future refinements.
